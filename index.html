    How to play

    Race basics

    1. Pick a racer, then a kart in the Garage.
    2. Choose a track and a difficulty, then start.
    3. Pass every checkpoint to make a lap count — cut the course and the lap is void.
    4. Finish top 3 to earn points and climb your local leaderboard.

    Driving tips

    • Tap Space mid-corner to break traction, then steer into the slide for a drift.
    • Drifting fills the boost meter — release for a short surge of speed.
    • Racing line beats top speed: brake before the corner, not in it.
    • Off-road costs serious speed. Barriers cost even more.

    Power-ups

    Nitro Boost

    Big speed surge for a few seconds.

    Energy Shield

    Absorbs the next hit and shoves rivals aside.

    Oil Slick

    Drops a hazard behind you that spins out anyone who hits it.

    Pulse Wave

    Disrupts nearby rivals, killing their speed for a moment.

    Every crate shows what it holds — colour, glyph and name tag. Drive through one to take it, then press Shift to use it. Crates respawn with a new power-up after a few seconds.

    Difficulty

    • Easy — slower, sloppier rivals, forgiving grip and collision recovery.
    • Medium — balanced rivals and standard physics.
    • Hard — faster, aggressive rivals, less grip assist, harsher mistakes.
